<h2>Selected Writing / Research</h2>

I occasionally write about the topics which interest me the most in crypto:

- [*Ethereum Reorgs after the Merge*](https://www.paradigm.xyz/2021/07/ethereum-reorgs-after-the-merge/), with [Vitalik Buterin](https://twitter.com/VitalikButerin): We investigate chain reorganizations in Proof of Work Ethereum and explain how they are affected by the Proof of Stake transition.

- [*Ethereum is a Dark Forest*](https://www.paradigm.xyz/2020/08/ethereum-is-a-dark-forest/), with [Dan Robinson](https://twitter.com/danrobinson): We narrate our experience trying to whitehat rescue $12,000 and failing due to being frontrun by a generalized Ethereum mempool bot, a horror story about MEV.

- [*On Staking Pools and Staking Derivatives*](https://www.paradigm.xyz/2021/04/on-staking-pools-and-staking-derivatives/), with [Hasu](https://twitter.com/hasufl): We unpack how decentralized staking pools and staking derivatives work, and their impact on their underlying Proof of Stake protocols

- [*(Almost) Everything you wanted to know about Optimistic Rollup*](https://www.paradigm.xyz/2021/01/almost-everything-you-need-to-know-about-optimistic-rollup/): Deriving Optimistic Rollups from first principles in a socratic dialogue.

- [*Ethereum Blockspace - Who gets What and Why*](https://www.paradigm.xyz/2021/03/ethereum-blockspace-who-gets-what-and-why/), with [Leo Zhang](https://twitter.com/Leorzhang): We analyze the evolving market dynamics around Ethereum's blockspace.

- *EIP1559 Series*, with [Hasu](https://twitter.com/hasufl): We [analyzed](https://www.paradigm.xyz/2020/06/analysis-of-eip-1559/) the base mechanics under EIP1559 & its main [alternative](https://www.paradigm.xyz/2020/06/analysis-of-eip-2593-escalator/), [bounded](https://www.paradigm.xyz/2021/02/establishing-bounds-for-miner-revenue-in-eip-1559/) the change in miner income as a result of 1559, and finally [predicted](https://www.paradigm.xyz/2021/02/miners-will-accept-eip-1559-here-is-why/) why the proposal would get activated without any problem (which [it did](https://www.cnbc.com/2021/08/05/ethereum-just-activated-its-london-hard-fork-and-its-a-big-deal.html), 6 months later).

<h2>Rust Software</h2>

I am a big proponent of writing software in the
<a href="https://www.rust-lang.org/">Rust programming language</a> for
safety, performance and portability:

- [`foundry`](https://github.com/gakonst/foundry): Foundry is a blazing fast, portable and modular toolkit for Ethereum application development. It is a rewrite of [`dapptools`](https://github.com/dapphub/dapptools)

- [`ethers-rs`](https://github.com/gakonst/ethers-rs/): Complete library and wallet implementation for interacting with EVM-compatible blockchains, focused in modularity and ergonomics.

- [`opensea-rs`](https://github.com/gakonst/opensea-rs/): Client to Opensea's APIs and Ethereum smart contracts with Fireblocks and Flashbots support.

- [`mev-inspect-rs`](https://github.com/gakonst/mev-inspect-rs/): Blazing fast historical MEV discoverer, the first backend of [MEV Explore](https://explore.flashbots.net/) (now replaced by [`mev-inspect-py`](https://github.com/flashbots/mev-inspect-py))

- [`darkforest-rs`](https://github.com/gakonst/darkforest-rs/) and [`ark-circom`](https://github.com/gakonst/ark-circom): Headless client to [Dark Forest](https://zkga.me), along with an [`arkworks`](https://github.com/arkworks-rs) <> [`circom`](https://github.com/iden3/circom) library.
